## Product Overview
**One-liner:** The AI-native audit OS for solo, small, and mid-sized firms — from client onboarding to final opinion, faster.

**What it does:** Fi371 automates the repetitive, structured work of an audit engagement — client onboarding, document requests, trial-balance/GL data ingestion, risk assessment, procedure drafting, evidence linking, and conclusion/opinion drafting — so audit teams "close engagements in days, not weeks." Every AI output is traceable and approved by a qualified auditor. It also includes purpose-built workflows for crypto / digital-asset audits.

**Product category:** Audit technology / audit automation software — positioned as an "audit OS" / AI-native audit platform.

**Product type:** B2B SaaS (web app at `app.fi371.com`).

**Business model:** Sales-led, demo-first. Pricing is intentionally **not public** — prospects book a demo, then pricing is quoted/custom per firm. Primary motion: Book Demo → scoping conversation → custom pricing.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Audit teams are still losing time to work that should already be automated — slowing engagements and squeezing margin.

**Specific pains (from the site):**
- Endless client follow-ups
- Manual document chasing across emails, drives, and portals
- Messy TB / GL data imports that need constant cleanup
- Repetitive procedure drafting for similar engagements
- Disconnected evidence scattered across tools and formats
- Slow review cycles / bottlenecks that delay completion
- New crypto audit pressure without clear workflows

**Why alternatives fall short:** Legacy tools help teams *organize* audit work but don't actively *move it forward* — they rely on manual coordination, template work, and static workflows, with limited or bolted-on AI traceability.

**What it costs them:** Weeks of elapsed time per engagement, lower margin, capped capacity, frustrated teams. ⚠️ CONFIRM any quantified cost.

**Emotional tension:** Frustration with legacy tools that "look like they were built in 2005"; anxiety about falling behind on AI and on crypto demand.
